Offset Print d.o.o., Primorska 84, 21000 Novi Sad, Serbia ("we", "us") operates this website. This page explains what personal data we collect and how we use it.

## What we collect

**Contact form.** When you send us a message through the contact form, we receive the name, email address and any other information you choose to share. The form is processed by our delivery partner Web3Forms, which forwards the message to `office@offsetprint.eu`. We use the message only to reply to you.

**Server logs.** Our hosting provider (Amazon CloudFront) records standard access logs (IP address, request URL, user agent, timestamp) for security and traffic analysis. Logs are retained for a limited period and not shared with third parties.

## What we don't do

We do not run advertising trackers, set marketing cookies or share your information with third parties for advertising purposes.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email `office@offsetprint.eu` and we will respond within 30 days.

## Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
